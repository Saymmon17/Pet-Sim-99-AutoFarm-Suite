## 🌌 Introduction

**Pet Paradigm 99 — Autonomous Companion Economy Toolkit** is a comprehensive scripting suite designed for players who want to experience the joy of discovery without the fatigue of manual repetition. Instead of clicking endlessly through egg openings, coin collection, and quest turn-ins, users can let an intelligent scheduler handle the rhythm while they focus on strategy, breeding decisions, and building the ultimate companion roster.

The repository originated from a simple observation: players invested hundreds of hours chasing rare companions but rarely stopped to enjoy the world around them. Our toolkit reintroduces that joy by automating the mundane and illuminating the meaningful.

This is not a shortcut — it is a reorientation. It is the difference between manually steering a ship and setting a course so the wind can do the work.

## 🧠 Why Pet Paradigm 99?

The Pet Paradigm 99 universe is one of the most dynamic companion-economy sandboxes ever assembled. With hundreds of hatchable creatures, seasonal events, rotating traders, and a booming player-driven marketplace, the ceiling for progression keeps rising. We built the toolkit around four philosophical pillars:

1. **Observability** — every automated action is logged, timestamps included.
2. **Reversibility** — every toggle can be flipped back in one gesture.
3. **Adaptability** — the scheduler adjusts to patches, event rotations, and latency spikes.
4. **Respect** — for player time, for shared environments, and for long-term account health.

## 🧩 Automation Modules

### 1. Hatch Orchestrator
The Hatch Orchestrator analyzes the drop tables of every egg currently available, weights them against your objective (rarity chase, event completion, or synergy breeding), and schedules openings at the optimal cadence. It respects cooldowns and monitors inventory for overflow.

### 2. Momentum Harmonizer
Rather than a blunt boost, the Momentum Harmonizer smooths movement pacing so companion actions feel natural. Players configure a target velocity ceiling, and the harmonizer interpolates in real time.

### 3. Wealth Cascade
The Wealth Cascade module harvests coins, dropped loot, and event tokens with a rotating priority queue. It reacts dynamically to nearby threats by temporarily pausing — because nothing is more frustrating than a lost loot pile.

### 4. Quest Synergy
Quests are mapped to a graph, and the Synergy module finds the shortest fulfillment path across your current roster. If a quest requires three rare companions, the module suggests the eggs most likely to yield them within your budget.

### 5. Ascension Conductor
Rank-ups require specific cumulative milestones. The Conductor tracks every counter and nudges you through the exact sequence needed to reach the next tier without over-farming.

### 6. Booth Radar
A passive listener that watches the marketplace for mispriced listings. When an item slips in below the median, the Radar surfaces a notification with a one-tap claim action.

## ⚡ Performance & Reliability

Performance is not a feature — it is the baseline. We profile every module weekly and enforce a strict budget:

| Metric | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Idle CPU | < 1.5% |
| Active CPU | < 6% |
| Memory (steady state) | < 180 MB |
| Startup time | < 900 ms |
| Recovery from disconnect | < 4 s |

Additional resilience measures include jittered scheduling (to avoid lockstep behavior), exponential backoff on retries, and graceful degradation when network conditions worsen.

Configuration is expressed in a single, human-readable manifest. Below is a sample illustration of some available knobs (values are illustrative only):

  name: "Nightshift"
  schedule: "22:00-06:00"

    enabled: true

    enabled: true

    enabled: false

    enabled: true

  level: "info"
  rotate_daily: true

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this modify anything outside the client?**  
No. Everything executes within the supported environment permissions.

**Can I pause everything with one action?**  
Yes — the global panic toggle halts all modules instantly.

**Will my progress be lost if the application crashes?**  
No. The session persistence layer writes checkpoints every thirty seconds.

**Is there a mobile-first mode?**  
Yes. The interface detects small viewports and switches to a compact layout.

**Do you support custom plugins?**  
Yes, a plugin schema is published in the documentation, allowing third-party modules with a consistent safety contract.

**Is there a customer support desk?**  
Absolutely. Our team answers inquiries 24/7 through the community hub.
